Admissions Information

Degree Programs

We offer BS in Computer Science, BS in Software Engineering, and MS in Computer Science.

Eligibility Criteria

Minimum 50% marks in Intermediate (Pre-Engineering / ICS) or equivalent with Mathematics.

Application Steps

  1. Fill out the online application portal registration.
  2. Upload necessary academic certificates and transcript records.
  3. Submit processing fee via designated bank challan.

Important Deadlines

Fall 2026 Intake: Applications close on August 15th, 2026.
